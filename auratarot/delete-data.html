How to delete your AuraTarot data

App: AuraTarot

Developer: Basanapps

Delete from the app

  1. Open AuraTarot and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Delete All My History and Local Data.
  3. Confirm. Deletion is immediate.

You can also uninstall the app: Android then removes all of its data from the phone.

What is deleted

Immediately and permanently:

  • All your saved readings (tarot and, if you are updating from an earlier version, palm readings).
  • Your profile: the name and date of birth you entered.
  • Your preferences: visual theme and reminder time.

Basanapps does not store any of this data on servers, so no copy remains outside your phone and there is no additional retention period.

Data handled by third parties

  • Google AdMob. The ad service uses the advertising ID and technical data about the device, and Google keeps them according to its Privacy Policy. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings, under Privacy → Ads.
